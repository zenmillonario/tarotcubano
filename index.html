Meet the Creator

The Journey Behind Tarot Cubano

Born in Havana, Cuba, my life has been shaped by two worlds. After beginning a new life in the United States, I spent years exploring history, spirituality, symbolism, and the timeless stories that connect cultures across generations.

That search eventually led me into the Amazon rainforest, where I participated in a traditional indigenous plant medicine initiation. The experience profoundly transformed the way I understood archetypes, mythology, nature, and the universal language of symbols. It became one chapter in a lifelong journey of discovery rather than its destination.

Over the years, I immersed myself in Cuban history, sacred traditions, folklore, religion, and ancestral memory. Tarot Cubano is the culmination of that journey—a collector's deck created to honor the resilience, beauty, and spiritual soul of Cuba through the timeless language of tarot.

My hope is that every card inspires reflection, meaningful conversation, and a deeper appreciation for the culture and stories that continue to shape the Cuban spirit.
